# H.R. 2018

> To ensure that the Metropolitan Washington Airports Authority complies with auditing standards.

## § 1. Auditing standards requirement

No Federal funds may be made available to the Metropolitan Washington Airports Authority unless the Secretary of the Department of Transportation certifies that the Metropolitan Washington Airports Authority is complying with appropriate auditing standards.
