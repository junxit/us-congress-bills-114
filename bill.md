# H.R. 5848

> To amend the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act to include individuals participating in pre-apprenticeship programs offered by employers and provide that such employers may be reimbursed under such Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Apprenticeship Improvement Act of 2016”.

## § 2. Inclusion of pre-apprenticeship programs

The Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act (29 U.S.C. 3101 et seq.) is amended—

- **(1)** in section 3(44), in the matter preceding subparagraph (A), by inserting “, including an apprentice or pre-apprentice,” after “paid participant”; and
- **(2)** in section 122(a)(2)(B), insert before the semicolon the following: “, except that for purposes of this Act, such programs may include programs that include pre-apprentices”.
