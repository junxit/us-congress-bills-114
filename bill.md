# H. CON. RES. 139

> Providing for a correction in the enrollment of H.R. 2577.

That in the enrollment of the bill H.R. 2577, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall amend the long title so as to read: “Making appropriations for military construction, the Department of Veterans Affairs, and related agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2017, and for other purposes.”.
