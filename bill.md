# H.R. 1645

> To amend title 49, United States Code, with respect to urbanized area formula grants, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Transportation for Elderly and Disabled Individuals Act of 2015”.

## § 2. Urbanized area formula grants

Section 5307(a)(2) of title 49, United States Code, is amended in subparagraphs (A) and (B), by inserting before “during” the following: “or general public demand response service”.
