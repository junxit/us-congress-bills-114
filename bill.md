# S. 2771

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to expand the qualifications for licensed mental health counselors of the Department of Veterans Affairs.

## § 1. Expansion of qualifications for licensed mental health counselors of the Department of Veterans Affairs

Section 7402(b)(11)(A) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by inserting “or equivalent degree” after “master's degree”.
