# S. 2525

> To increase the number of States that may conduct Medicaid demonstration programs to improve access to community mental health services.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Expand Excellence in Mental Health Act”.

## § 2. Community mental health services demonstration program

Section 223(d)(3) of the Protecting Access to Medicare Act of 2014 (42 U.S.C. 1396a note) is amended by striking “8” and inserting “24”.
