# S. 3512

> To reauthorize the Historically Black Colleges and Universities Historic Preservation Program.

## § 1. Historically Black Colleges and Universities Historic Preservation Program reauthorized

Section 507(d)(2) of division I of the Omnibus Parks and Public Lands Management Act of 1996 (54 U.S.C. 302101 note; Public Law 104–333) is amended by striking the period at the end and inserting “and each of fiscal years 2017 through 2023.”.
