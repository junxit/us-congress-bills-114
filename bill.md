# H.R. 6351

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend for one year the credit for new qualified fuel cell motor vehicles.

## § 1. Extension of credit for new qualified fuel cell motor vehicles

- **(a)** *In general* Paragraph (1) of section 30B(k) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “December 31, 2016” and inserting “December 31, 2017”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to property placed in service after December 31, 2016.
