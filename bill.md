# H.R. 1991

> To extend the authority of the Secretary of the Interior and the Secretary of Agriculture to carry out the Federal Lands Recreation Enhancement Act, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Federal Lands Recreation Enhancement Act Extension Act of 2015”.

## § 2. Extension of authority under Federal Lands Recreation Enhancement Act

Section 810 of the Federal Lands Recreation Enhancement Act (16 U.S.C. 6809) is amended to read as follows:

> ## § 810. Sunset provision
>
> The authority of the Secretary to carry out this title shall terminate on September 30, 2017.
