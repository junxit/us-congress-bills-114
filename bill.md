# H.R. 5890

> To promote stability and security in the Asia-Pacific maritime domains, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Asia-Pacific Maritime Security Initiative Act of 2016”.

## § 2. Appropriate committees of Congress defined

In this Act, the term appropriate committees of Congress means—

- **(1)** the Committee on Foreign Relations, the Committee on Armed Services, and the Committee on Appropriations of the Senate; and
- **(2)** the Committee on Foreign Affairs, the Committee on Armed Services, and the Committee on Appropriations of the House of Representatives.
