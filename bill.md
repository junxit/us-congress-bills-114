# H.R. 725

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to repeal the estate tax and retain stepped-up basis at death.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Permanently Repeal the Estate Tax Act of 2015”.

## § 2. Repeal of estate tax and retention of basis step-up

Effective for estates of decedents dying after December 31, 2014, chapter 11 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is repealed.
