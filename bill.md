# H.R. 1330

> To amend the Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act to require the Secretary of the Interior to conduct offshore oil and gas leasing, to use revenues from such leasing to capitalize bonds that provide a dedicated source of revenue to fund highway, other transportation, and water infrastructure projects, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “American-Made Energy and Infrastructure Jobs Act”.
