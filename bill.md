# H.R. 2765

> To prohibit the National Science Foundation from obligating amounts for the Polar Learning and Responding Climate Change Educational Partnership.

## § 1. Funding prohibition

The National Science Foundation may not obligate any amounts on or after the date of enactment of this Act for the Polar Learning and Responding Climate Change Educational Partnership.
