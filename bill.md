# S. 3232

> To revise repayment terms for certain loans made under the Lowell National Historical Park Historic Preservation Loan Program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Lowell National Historical Park Creative Partnerships Act”.

## § 2. Reauthorization

Section 303(a)(1) of the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the establishment of the Lowell National Historical Park in the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, and for other purposes”, approved June 5, 1978 (16 U.S.C. 410cc–33(a)(1)), is amended in the first sentence by striking “thirty-five years” and inserting “35 years, except that any loan made in 1984 shall have a maturity of 42 years”.
