# S. 312

> To amend the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 regarding school libraries, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Strengthening Kids' Interest in Learning and Libraries Act” or the “SKILLS Act”.

## § 2. References

Except as otherwise expressly provided, wherever in this Act an amendment or repeal is expressed in terms of an amendment to, or repeal of, a section or other provision, the reference shall be considered to be made to a section or other provision of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 6301 et seq.).
