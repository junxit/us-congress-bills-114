# S. 2743

> To amend the Agricultural Act of 2014 to repeal a loophole for payment limitations.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Farm Payment Loophole Elimination Act of 2016”.

## § 2. Repeal

- **(a)** *In general* Section 1604 of the Agricultural Act of 2014 (7 U.S.C. 1308–1 note; Public Law 113–79) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking subsection (c); and
  - **(2)** by redesignating subsections (d) through (g) as subsections (c) through (f), respectively.
- **(b)** *Application* The Secretary of Agriculture shall apply the amendments made by subsection (a) beginning with the 2017 crop year.
