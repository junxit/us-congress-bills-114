# H.R. 2107

> To amend title XIX of the Social Security Act to clarify the treatment of certain Medicaid enrollment brokers.

## § 1. Treatment of certain Medicaid brokers

Section 1903(b)(4) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1396b(b)(4)) is amended—

- **(1)** in the matter before subparagraph (A), by inserting after “respect to the broker” the following: “(or, in the case of subparagraph (A) or subparagraph (B)(i), if the Secretary finds that the broker has established and maintains policies and procedures to ensure the independence of its enrollment activities from the interests of any managed care entity or provider)”; and
- **(2)** in subparagraph (B)—
  - **(A)** by inserting “(i)” after “either”; and
  - **(B)** by inserting “(ii)” after “health care provider or”.
