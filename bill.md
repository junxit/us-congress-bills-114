# S. 1918

> To amend the Endangered Species Act of 1973 to extend the import- and export-related provision of that Act to species proposed for listing as threatened or endangered under that Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Conserving Ecosystems by Ceasing the Importation of Large (CECIL) Animal Trophies Act”.

## § 2. Amendment to Endangered Species Act of 1973

Section 9(a)(1) of the Endangered Species Act of 1973 (16 U.S.C. 1538(a)(1)) is amended by striking subparagraph (A) and inserting the following:

> - **(A)** for any such species listed, or proposed to be listed, under section 4, import the species into, or export the species from, the United States;
