# H.R. 5151

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to provide an extension of the special survivor indemnity allowance provided to widows and widowers of certain deceased members of the uniformed services.

## § 1. Extension of special survivor indemnity allowance

- **(a)** *Payment amount per fiscal year* Paragraph (2)(I) of section 1450(m) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by striking “fiscal year 2017” and inserting “fiscal years 2017 and 2018”.
- **(b)** *Duration* Paragraph (6) of such section is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “September 30, 2017” and inserting “September 30, 2018”; and
  - **(2)** by striking “October 1, 2017” each place it appears and inserting “October 1, 2018”.
