# H.R. 6474

> To eliminate the daily newspaper cross-ownership rule of the Federal Communications Commission.

## § 1. Elimination of daily newspaper cross-ownership rule

- **(a)** *No force or effect* Paragraph (d) of section 73.3555 of title 47, Code of Federal Regulations, shall have no force or effect after the date of the enactment of this Act.
- **(b)** *Removal from rules* Not later than 1 year after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Federal Communications Commission shall complete all actions necessary to remove such paragraph from its rules.
