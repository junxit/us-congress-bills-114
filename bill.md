# H.R. 228

> To amend title I of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 to extend the authorization of the Bulletproof Vest Partnership Grant Program through fiscal year 2018.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Bulletproof Vest Partnership Grant Act of 2015”.

## § 2. Extension of authorization of appropriations for bulletproof vest partnership grant program

Section 1001(a)(23) of title I of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (42 U.S.C. 3793(a)(23)) is amended by striking “2012” and inserting “2018”.
