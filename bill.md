# H.R. 4113

> To amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 regarding reasonable break time for nursing mothers.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Supporting Working Moms Act of 2015”.

## § 2. Reasonable break time for nursing mothers

Section 13(a) of the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 (29 U.S.C. 213(a)) is amended by striking “sections 6 (except section 6(d) in the case of paragraph (1) of this subsection) and 7” and inserting “sections 6 and 7 (except sections 6(d) and 7(r) in the case of paragraph (1) of this subsection)”.
