# S. 1461

> To provide for the extension of the enforcement instruction on supervision requirements for outpatient therapeutic services in critical access and small rural hospitals through 2015.

## § 1. Extension of enforcement instruction on supervision requirements for outpatient therapeutic services in critical access and small rural hospitals through 2015

Section 1 of Public Law 113–198 is amended—

- **(1)** in the section heading, by inserting “and 2015” after “2014”; and
- **(2)** by striking “calendar year 2014” and inserting “calendar years 2014 and 2015”.
