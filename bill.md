# S. 2191

> To establish Federal-State higher education financing partnerships to drive down the cost of tuition for millions of American students.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Promoting Access and Retention Through New Efforts to Require Shared Higher Investments in Postsecondary Success Act” or the “PARTNERSHIPS Act”.
