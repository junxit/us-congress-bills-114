# H.J. RES. 70

> Disapproving a rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to “National Ambient Air Quality Standards for Ozone”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to “National Ambient Air Quality Standards for Ozone” (contained in the docket numbered EPA–HQ–OAR–2008–0699), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
