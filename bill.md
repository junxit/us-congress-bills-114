# H.R. 5207

> To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to provide that an alien may not be denied admission to the United States because of the alien’s religion, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Freedom of Religion Act of 2016”.

## § 2. Prohibition on denying admission because of religion

Chapter 2 of title I of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1181 et seq.) is amended by inserting after section 219 the following:

> ## § 220. Prohibition on denying admission because of religion
>
> Notwithstanding any other provision of the immigration laws, an alien may not be denied admission to the United States because of the alien’s religion or lack of religious beliefs.
