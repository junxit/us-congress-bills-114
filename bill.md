# H. RES. 769

> Terminating a Select Investigative Panel of the Committee on Energy and Commerce.

That the Select Investigative Panel of the Committee on Energy and Commerce established pursuant to House Resolution 461, agreed to October 7, 2015, is hereby terminated.
