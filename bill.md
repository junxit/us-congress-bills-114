# H.R. 6525

> To amend the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 to require students to undergo lead screenings.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Lead-free Students Act”.

## § 2. Lead screening

Subpart 2 of part F of title VIII of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 7901 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> ## § 8549D. Lead screening requirement
>
> As a condition of receiving funds under this Act, a local educational agency shall require that prior to enrolling in an elementary school for the first time, a student shall undergo a screening of the student’s blood lead level.
