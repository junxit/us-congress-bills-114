# H.R. 5162

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to authorize the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to disclose to non-Department of Veterans Affairs health care providers certain medical records of veterans who receive health care from such providers.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Vet Connect Act of 2016”.

## § 2. Authority to disclose certain medical records of veterans who receive non-Department of Veterans Affairs health care

Section 7332(b)(2) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new subparagraph:

> - **(H)** To a non-Department entity (including private entities and other departments or agencies of the Federal Government) that provides hospital care or medical treatment to veterans.
