# S. 1152

> To make permanent the extended period of protections for members of uniformed services relating to mortgages, mortgage foreclosure, and eviction, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Foreclosure Relief and Extension for Servicemembers Act of 2015”.

## § 2. Making permanent extended period of protections for members of uniformed services relating to mortgages, mortgage foreclosure, and eviction

Section 710(d) of the Honoring America's Veterans and Caring for Camp Lejeune Families Act of 2012 (Public Law 112–154) is amended by striking paragraphs (1) and (3).
