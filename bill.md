# H.R. 4032

> To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to provide for a limitation on the resettlement of refugees.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “States’ Right of Refugee Refusal Act of 2015”.

## § 2. Limitation on resettlement

Section 412 of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1522) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(g)** *Limitation on resettlement* Notwithstanding any other provision of this section, the Director or the Federal agency administering subsection (b)(1) may not resettle any refugee or coordinate placement of any refugee in a State when that State government has communicated to the Director that the State government does not accede to the resettlement or placement of refugees in that State.
