# H.R. 4306

> To make permanent the Advisory Committee on Minority Veterans.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Advisory Committee on Minority Veterans Act of 2015”.

## § 2. Advisory Committee on Minority Veterans made permanent

Section 544 of title 38 of the United States Code is amended by striking “(e) The Committee shall cease to exist December 31, 2016.”.
