# S. 3506

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make technical corrections, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; table of contents; etc

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Tax Technical Corrections Act of 2016”.
- **(b)** *Amendment of Internal Revenue Code of 1986* Except as otherwise expressly provided, whenever in this Act an amendment or repeal is expressed in terms of an amendment to, or repeal of, a section or other provision, the reference shall be considered to be made to a section or other provision of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986.
