# S. 2518

> To authorize the use of Ebola funds for Zika response and preparedness.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Zika Response and Safety Act of 2016”.

## § 2. Authorization to use Ebola funds to fight Zika

- **(a)** *Authorization* In addition to the purposes otherwise provided for Ebola funds made available in prior appropriation Acts, such funds may also be used for Zika virus response and preparedness.
- **(b)** *Ebola funds defined* For purposes of this section, the term Ebola funds means amounts made available to any Department or agency for Ebola response and preparedness.
