# S. 2550

> To repeal the jury duty exemption for elected officials of the legislative branch.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Civic Duty Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of jury duty exemption for legislative branch officials

Section 310 of the Legislative Branch Appropriations Act, 1991 (2 U.S.C. 30a) is repealed.
