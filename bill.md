# S. 805

> To amend title 54, United States Code, to make Hispanic-serving institutions eligible for technical and financial assistance for the establishment of preservation training and degree programs.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Preservation Research at Institutions Serving Minorities Act of 2015” or the “PRISM Act of 2015”.

## § 2. Eligibility of Hispanic-serving institutions for assistance for preservation education and training programs

Section 303903(3) of title 54, United States Code, is amended by inserting “to Hispanic-serving institutions (as defined in section 502(a) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1101a(a))),” after “universities,”.
