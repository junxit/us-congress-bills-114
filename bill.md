# H.R. 2934

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to extend the authority of the Advisory Committee on Homeless Veterans for five years.

## § 1. Five-year extension of Advisory Committee on Homeless Veterans

Section 2066(d) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “December 31, 2015” and inserting “December 31, 2020”.
