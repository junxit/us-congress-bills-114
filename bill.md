# H. RES. 357

> Expressing the sense of the House of Representatives regarding the need to eliminate partisan redistricting and gerrymandering.

## § 1. Eliminating partisan redistricting and gerrymandering

It is the sense of the House of Representatives that—

- **(1)** Congress should establish a Federal reapportionment system that would create compact and contiguous congressional districts that, to the greatest extent possible, follow geographic boundaries and county lines;
- **(2)** congressional districts should not favor or disfavor an incumbent or political party;
- **(3)** congressional districts should adhere to the existing standards of equal population; and
- **(4)** the highest court of each State should have the power to reject congressional district maps that do not meet the above criteria.
