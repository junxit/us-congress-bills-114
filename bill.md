# H.J. RES. 68

> Disapproving a rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to “Standards of Performance for Greenhouse Gas Emissions from New, Modified, and Reconstructed Stationary Sources: Electric Utility Generating Units”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to “Standards of Performance for Greenhouse Gas Emissions from New, Modified, and Reconstructed Stationary Sources: Electric Utility Generating Units”, and contained in the dockets numbered EPA–HQ–OAR–2013–0495 and EPA–HQ–OAR–2013–0603, and such rule shall have no force or effect.
