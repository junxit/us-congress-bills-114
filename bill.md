# H.R. 221

> To prevent a taxpayer bailout of health insurance issuers.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Obamacare Taxpayer Bailout Prevention Act”.

## § 2. Prevention of bailout of health insurance issuers

The Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act is amended by striking section 1342 (42 U.S.C. 18062).
