# H.R. 2678

> To prohibit United States contributions to the United Nations Population Fund.

## § 1. Prohibition on United States contributions to the United Nations Population Fund

No funds available to the Department of State or any other department or agency may be used to provide contributions directly or indirectly to the United Nations Population Fund.
