# S. 2620

> To facilitate the addition of park administration at the Coltsville National Historical Park, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Amendment to Coltsville National Historical Park donation site

Section 3032(b)(2)(B) of the Carl Levin and Howard P. “Buck” McKeon National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2015 (16 U.S.C. 410qqq(b)(2)(B)) is amended by striking “East Armory” and inserting “Colt Armory Complex”.
