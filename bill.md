# H.R. 5929

> To amend the Occupational Safety and Health Act of 1970 to require employers to provide menstrual hygiene products for employees.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Menstrual Products for Employees Act”.

## § 2. Menstrual hygiene products

Section 6 of the Occupational Safety and Health Act of 1970 (29 U.S.C. 655) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(g)** The Secretary shall by rule promulgate a requirement that each employer with not less than 100 employees provide menstrual hygiene products free of charge for employees of the employer.
