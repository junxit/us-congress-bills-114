# H.R. 6074

> To amend title 31, United States Code, to limit the payments from the Judgment Fund in certain cases, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stop Taxpayer Obamacare Payouts ‘STOP’ Act of 2016”.

## § 2. No payments from Judgment Fund for risk corridor cases

Section 1304 of title 31, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(d)** Notwithstanding any other provision of this section, no amount may be paid from the appropriation made by this section for any claim pertaining to the program under section 1342 of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act (42 U.S.C. 18062).
