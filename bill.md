# H.R. 6365

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend for one year the credit for energy efficient new homes.

## § 1. Extension of credit for energy efficient new homes

- **(a)** *In general* Subsection (g) of section 45L of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “December 31, 2016” and inserting “December 31, 2017”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to homes acquired after December 31, 2016.
