# H.R. 3133

> Relating to certain Indian land-related takings claims of the Grand Traverse Band of Ottawa and Chippewa Indians of Michigan and its individual members.

## § 1. Congressional reference and report

Pursuant to the findings and conclusions contained in the Report issued by the chief judge of the U.S. Court of Federal Claims, the Secretary of the Treasury shall pay, out of money not otherwise appropriated, to the Grand Traverse Band of Ottawa and Chippewa Indians of Michigan for the benefit of itself and its members, the sum of $____.
