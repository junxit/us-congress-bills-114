# H.R. 3124

> To permit the expungement of records of certain marijuana-related offenses.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Clean Slate for Marijuana Offenses Act of 2015”.

- **(c)** *Effective Date* The amendments made by this Act apply to individuals convicted of an offense before, on, or after the date of enactment of this Act.
