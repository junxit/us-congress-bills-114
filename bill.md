# S. 1720

> To require the Secretary of the Treasury to redesign $10 Federal reserve notes so as to include a likeness of Harriet Tubman, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Harriet Tubman Currency Tribute Act of 2015”.

## § 2. Likeness of Harriet Tubman required to be included on the face of $10 Federal reserve notes

The eighth undesignated paragraph of section 16 of the Federal Reserve Act (12 U.S.C. 418) is amended by adding at the end the following: “The Secretary of the Treasury shall ensure that the face of all $10 Federal reserve notes printed after December 31, 2020, shall bear the likeness of Harriet Tubman.”.
