# S. 2081

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to extend authorities for the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to expand presumption of service connection for compensation for diseases the Secretary determines are associated with exposure to herbicide agents, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Extension of authorities of Secretary of Veterans Affairs relating to presumption of service connection for compensation for diseases associated with exposure to herbicide agents

Section 1116(e) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “September 30, 2015” and inserting “September 30, 2030”.
