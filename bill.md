# S. 3151

> To enhance investment in education and employment programs by eliminating duplication, cutting red tape, and increasing flexibility, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Cutting Administrative Red-tape for Education and Employment Reinvestment Act” or the “CAREER Act”.
