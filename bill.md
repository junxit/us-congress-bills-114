# S. 1048

> To remove the authority of the Secretary of Energy to amend or issue new energy efficiency standards for ceiling fans.

## § 1. Ceiling fan standards

Section 325(ff) of the Energy Policy and Conservation Act (42 U.S.C. 6295(ff)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking paragraphs (5) and (6); and
- **(2)** by redesignating paragraph (7) as paragraph (5).
