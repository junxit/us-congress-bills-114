# H.R. 3610

> To amend the Investment Company Act of 1940 to terminate the exemption of companies located in Puerto Rico, the Virgin Islands, and any other possession of the United States.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Puerto Rico Investor Protection Act of 2015”.

## § 2. Termination of exemption

Section 6(a) of the Investment Company Act of 1940 (15 U.S.C. 80a–6(a)) is amended by striking paragraph (1).
