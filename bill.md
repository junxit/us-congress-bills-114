# H.R. 6383

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend for two years the credit for qualified microturbine property.

## § 1. Extension of credit for qualified microturbine property

- **(a)** *In general* Subsection (c)(2)(D) of section 48 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “December 31, 2016” and inserting “December 31, 2018”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to periods after December 31, 2016.
