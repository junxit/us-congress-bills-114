# H. CON. RES. 174

> Directing the Clerk of the House of Representatives to make a correction in the enrollment of H.R. 34.

That in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 34) to authorize and strengthen the tsunami detection, forecast, warning, research, and mitigation program of the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration, and for other purposes, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following correction: Amend the long title so as to read: “An Act to accelerate the discovery, development, and delivery of 21st century cures, and for other purposes.”.
