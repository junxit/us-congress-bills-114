# H.R. 1688

> To amend the Veterans Access, Choice, and Accountability Act of 2014 to designate 20 graduate medical education residency positions specifically for the study of optometry.

## § 1. Graduate medical education residency positions in optometry

Section 301(b)(2)(A) of the Veterans Access, Choice, and Accountability Act of 2014 (Public Law 113–146; 38 U.S.C. 7302 note) is amended by striking “positions.” and inserting “positions, including up to 20 positions specifically designated for residencies in optometry.”.
