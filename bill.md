# H.R. 4928

> To amend chapter 44 of title 18, United States Code, to amend the requirement that interstate firearms sales by Federal firearms licensees be made in accordance with the State law where the transaction occurs.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Firearms Transfer Improvement Act”.

## § 2. Amendment of requirement that interstate firearms sales by Federal firearms licensees be made in accordance with the State law where the transaction occurs

Section 922(b)(3)(A) of title 18, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “rifle or shotgun” and inserting “firearm”;
- **(2)** by striking “both such States” and inserting “the State in which the licensee’s place of business is located”; and
- **(3)** by striking “both States” and inserting “the State in which the licensee’s place of business is located”.
