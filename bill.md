# S. 1321

> To expand benefits to the families of public safety officers who suffer fatal climate-related injuries sustained in the line of duty and proximately resulting in death.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fairness for Fallen Officers Act of 2015”.

## § 2. Benefits for families of public safety officers who suffer fatal climate-related injuries

Section 1201 of title I of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (42 U.S.C. 3796) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (b), by inserting “or climate-related injury (as defined in subsection (l))” after “personal injury”;
- **(2)** in subsection (k)—
  - **(A)** in the matter preceding paragraph (1), by striking “or vascular rupture” and inserting “vascular rupture, or climate-related injury”;
  - **(B)** in paragraph (1)(A), by inserting “exposure to extreme environmental conditions,” after “disaster relief,”;
  - **(C)** in paragraph (2), by striking “or vascular rupture” and inserting “vascular rupture, or climate-related injury”;
  - **(D)** in paragraph (3), by striking “or vascular rupture” and inserting “vascular rupture, or climate-related injury”; and
  - **(E)** in the flush text following paragraph (3), by striking “or vascular rupture” and inserting “vascular rupture, or climate-related injury”; and
- **(3)** by amending subsection (l) to read as follows:
  > - **(l)** For purposes of subsection (k)—
  >   - **(1)** the term climate-related injury means an injury that is caused by an environmental condition experienced by an officer, including hypothermia, heatstroke, and hyperthermia; and
  >   - **(2)** the term nonroutine stressful or strenuous physical excludes actions of a clerical, administrative, or nonmanual nature.
