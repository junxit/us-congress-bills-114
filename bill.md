# H.R. 4584

> To amend the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 to authorize COPS grantees to use grant funds for active shooter training, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “POLICE Act of 2016”.

## § 2. Additional authorized use of COPS funds

Section 1701(b) of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (42 U.S.C. 3796dd(b)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (16), by striking “; and” at the end;
- **(2)** in paragraph (17), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(18)** to participate in nationally-recognized active shooter training programs that offer scenario-based, integrated response courses designed to counter active shooter threats or acts of terrorism against individuals or facilities.
