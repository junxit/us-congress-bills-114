# H.R. 88

> To amend subtitle IV of title 40, United States Code, regarding county additions to the Appalachian region.

## § 1. Additions to appalachian region

Section 14102(a)(1)(K) of title 40, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by inserting “Hickman,” after “Hawkins,”;
- **(2)** by inserting “Perry,” after “Overton,”; and
- **(3)** by inserting “Wayne,” after “Washington,”.
