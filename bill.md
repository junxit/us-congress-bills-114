# S. 496

> To prohibit the use of any Federal funds to finalize, implement, or enforce the proposed rule entitled “Standards for the Growing, Harvesting, Packing, and Holding of Produce for Human Consumption”.

## § 1. Prohibition against finalizing, implementing, or enforcing the proposed rule entitled “Standards for the Growing, Harvesting, Packing, and Holding of Produce for Human Consumption”

No Federal funds may be used to finalize, implement, or enforce the proposed rule entitled “Standards for the Growing, Harvesting, Packing, and Holding of Produce for Human Consumption” published by the Department of Health and Human Services on January 16, 2013 (78 Fed. Reg. 3503), or any successor or substantially similar rule.
