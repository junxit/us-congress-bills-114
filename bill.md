# S. 1090

> To amend the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act to provide eligibility for broadcasting facilities to receive certain disaster assistance, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Emergency Information Improvement Act of 2015”.

## § 2. Eligibility of broadcasting facilities for certain disaster assistance

- **(a)** *Private nonprofit facility defined* Section 102(11)(B) of the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. 5122(11)(B)) is amended by inserting “broadcasting facilities,” after “workshops,”.
- **(b)** *Critical services defined* Section 406(a)(3)(B) of the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. 5172(a)(3)(B)) is amended by striking “communications,” and inserting “communications (including broadcast and telecommunications),”.
