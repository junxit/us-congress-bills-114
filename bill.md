# H.R. 4380

> To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to remove limitations on the ability of certain dual citizens from participating in the Visa Waiver Program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Equal Protection in Travel Act of 2016”.

## § 2. Removal of limitations of certain dual citizens from participating in the Visa Waiver Program

Section 217(a)(12) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1187(a)(12)) is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (A)—
  - **(A)** by striking clause (ii);
  - **(B)** by striking “(C)—” and all that follows through “the alien has not been present” and inserting “(C), the alien has not been present”; and
  - **(C)** by redesignating subclauses (I), (II), and (III) as clauses (i), (ii), and (iii); and
- **(2)** in subparagraph (B), in the matter preceding clause (i), by striking “(A)(i)” and inserting “(A)”.
