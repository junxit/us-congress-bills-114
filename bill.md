# H.R. 3132

> To increase the amount of funding available for fiscal year 2015 for certain general business loans authorized under the Small Business Act.

## § 1. Increase to the amount of funding available to the Small Business Administration to make section 7(a) loans

The third proviso under the heading “Small Business Administration—Business Loans Program Account” in title V of division E of the Consolidated and Further Continuing Appropriations Act, 2015 (Public Law 113–235; 128 Stat. 2371) is amended by striking “$18,750,000,000” and inserting “$23,500,000,000”.
