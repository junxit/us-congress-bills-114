# S. 1791

> To amend the Delta Development Act to include Vernon and Sabine parishes in the definition of the term “Lower Mississippi”.

## § 1. Vernon and Sabine parishes

Section 4(2)(D) of the Delta Development Act (42 U.S.C. 3121 note; Public Law 100–460) is amended by inserting “Vernon, Sabine,” after “Webster”.
