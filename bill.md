# S. 438

> To provide for the repair, replacement, and maintenance of certain Indian irrigation projects.

## § 1. Short title; table of contents

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Irrigation Rehabilitation and Renovation for Indian Tribal Governments and Their Economies Act” or the “IRRIGATE Act”.

## § 2. Definitions

In this Act:

- **(1)** *Fund* The term Fund means the Indian Irrigation Fund established by section 101.
- **(2)** *Indian tribe* The term Indian tribe has the meaning given the term in section 4 of the Indian Self-Determination and Education Assistance Act (25 U.S.C. 450b).
- **(3)** *Secretary* The term Secretary means the Secretary of the Interior, acting through the Commissioner of Reclamation.
