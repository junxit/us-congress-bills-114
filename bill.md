# H.J. RES. 67

> Disapproving a rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to “Carbon Pollution Emission Guidelines for Existing Stationary Sources: Electric Utility Generating Units”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to “Carbon Pollution Emission Guidelines for Existing Stationary Sources: Electric Utility Generating Units”, and contained in the docket numbered EPA–HQ–OAR–2013–0602, and such rule shall have no force or effect.
