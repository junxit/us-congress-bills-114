# S. 1644

> To permanently extend the private mortgage insurance tax deduction.

## § 1. Permanent extension of deduction for mortgage insurance premiums

- **(a)** *In general* Subparagraph (E) of section 163(h)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking clause (iv).
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to amounts paid or accrued after December 31, 2014, and to amounts properly allocable to periods after such date.
