# H.R. 3891

> To amend the Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002 to exempt issuers with a total market capitalization of less than $2,000,000,000 from the auditor attestation requirement for internal control assessments.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Get Regulation Out of the Way Act of 2015” or the “GROW Act of 2015”.

## § 2. Additional exemption from auditor attestation to internal control assessments

Section 404(c) of the Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002 (15 U.S.C. 7262(c)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking the period at the end and inserting “; or”;
- **(2)** by striking “prepared for an issuer” and inserting the following:
  > prepared for—
  > - **(1)** an issuer

- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(2)** an issuer that, as of the first day of the fiscal year to which the assessment described in such subsection relates, has a total market capitalization of less than $2,000,000,000.
