# H.R. 2118

> To amend the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States to extend to 2027 the production certificate program that allows refunds of duties on certain articles produced in United States insular possessions.

## § 1. Extension of production certificate program for certain products of United States insular possessions

Additional U.S. Note 5(h)(i) to chapter 91 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended by striking “2015” and inserting “2027”.
