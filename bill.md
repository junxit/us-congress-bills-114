# S. 3029

> To extend the authorization of appropriations to the Department of Veterans Affairs for purposes of awarding grants to veterans service organizations for the transportation of highly rural veterans.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Maurice (Mo) Bailey Highly Rural Veteran Transportation Grant Reauthorization Act of 2016”.

## § 2. Extension of authorization of appropriations for grants to veterans service organizations for transportation of highly rural veterans

Section 307(d) of the Caregivers and Veterans Omnibus Health Services Act of 2010 (Public Law 111–163; 124 Stat. 1154; 38 U.S.C. 1710 note) is amended by striking “2016” and inserting “2017”.
