# H.R. 2160

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend for one year the employer wage credit for employees who are active duty members of the uniformed services.

## § 1. EXTENSION OF EMPLOYER WAGE CREDIT FOR EMPLOYEES WHO ARE ACTIVE DUTY MEMBERS OF THE UNIFORMED SERVICES

- **(a)** *In General* Subsection (f) of section 45P of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “December 31, 2014” and inserting “December 31, 2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective Date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to payments made after December 31, 2014.
