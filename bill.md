# S. 1531

> To reform the provision of health insurance coverage by promoting health savings accounts, State-based alternatives to coverage under the Affordable Care Act, and price transparency, in order to promote a more market-based health care system, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; table of contents

## § 2. Sense of Congress

It is the sense of Congress that there is a need for legislation providing temporary transition funding for those who lose health insurance subsidies in the aftermath of a Supreme Court decision in favor of the plaintiffs-appellants in the case of King v. Burwell.
