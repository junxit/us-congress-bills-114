# S. 3317

> To prohibit the further extension or establishment of national monuments in the State of Utah except by express authorization of Congress.

## § 1. Limitation on further extension or establishment of National Monuments in Utah

Section 320301(d) of title 54, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in the subsection heading, by inserting “or Utah” after “Wyoming”; and
- **(2)** by inserting “or Utah” after “Wyoming”.
