# H.R. 823

> To better integrate STEM education into elementary and secondary instruction and curricula, to encourage high-quality STEM professional development, and to expand current mathematics and science education research to include engineering education.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Educating Tomorrow’s Engineers Act of 2015”.
