# H.R. 5923

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to authorize the Secretary of Health and Human Services to make grants to eligible entities to train elementary and secondary school nurses on how to respond to a biological or chemical attack or an outbreak of pandemic illness in a school building or on school grounds.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “School Protection Act of 2016”.
