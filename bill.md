# H.R. 6367

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend for one year the mine rescue team training credit.

## § 1. Extension of credit for mine rescue team training

- **(a)** *In general* Subsection (e) of section 45N of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “December 31, 2016” and inserting “December 31, 2017”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to taxable years after December 31, 2016.
