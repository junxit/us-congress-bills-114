# S. 2883

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to extend the requirement of the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to submit a report on the capacity of the Department of Veterans Affairs to provide for the specialized treatment and rehabilitative needs of disabled veterans.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Appropriate Care for Disabled Veterans Act of 2016”.

## § 2. Extension of requirement for report on capacity of Department of Veterans Affairs to provide for the specialized treatment and rehabilitative needs of disabled veterans

Section 1706(b)(5)(A) of title 38, United States Code, is amended, in the first sentence, by striking “through 2008”.
