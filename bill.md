# H.R. 2239

> To amend the Export-Import Bank Act of 1945 to increase the target financing of exports by small business concerns.

## § 1. Financing targets of small business concerns

Section 2(b)(1)(E)(v) of the Export-Import Bank Act of 1945 (12 U.S.C. 635(b)(1)(E)(v)) is amended by striking “20 percent” and inserting “25 percent”.
