# H.R. 1092

> To designate the Federal building located at 2030 Southwest 145th Avenue in Miramar, Florida, as the “Benjamin P. Grogan and Jerry L. Dove Federal Building”.

## § 1. Designation

The Federal building located at 2030 Southwest 145th Avenue in Miramar, Florida, shall be known and designated as the “Benjamin P. Grogan and Jerry L. Dove Federal Building”.

## § 2. References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Benjamin P. Grogan and Jerry L. Dove Federal Building”.
