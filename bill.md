# H.J. RES. 42

> Disapproving the rule submitted by the Federal Communications Commission relating to the matter of protecting and promoting the open Internet.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Federal Communications Commission relating to the matter of protecting and promoting the open Internet (Report and Order on Remand, Declaratory Ruling, and Order, adopted by the Commission on February 26, 2015) (FCC 15–24), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
