# S. 1065

> To amend title IV of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 to provide grants for the development of asthma management plans and the purchase of asthma inhalers and spacers for emergency use, as necessary.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “School Asthma Management Plan Act”.
