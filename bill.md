# H.R. 5524

> To repeal the Legal Services Corporation Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Legal Services Corporation Repeal Act of 2016”.

## § 2. Repeal

The Legal Services Corporation Act (42 U.S.C. 2996 et seq.) is repealed.
