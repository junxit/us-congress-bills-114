# H.R. 4877

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 3130 Grants Lake Boulevard in Sugar Land, Texas, as the “LCpl Garrett W. Gamble, USMC Post Office Building”.

## § 1. LCpl Garrett W. Gamble, USMC Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 3130 Grants Lake Boulevard in Sugar Land, Texas, shall be known and designated as the “LCpl Garrett W. Gamble, USMC Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “LCpl Garrett W. Gamble, USMC Post Office Building”.
