# H.R. 5354

> To amend title IV of the Social Security Act to improve supports for kinship caregivers in child welfare programs and the program of block grants to States for temporary assistance for needy families.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Improving Supports for Kinship Caregivers Act of 2016”.

## § 2. Effective date

The amendments made by this Act shall take effect on the 1st day of the 1st fiscal year beginning on or after the date of the enactment of this Act.
