# H.R. 5378

> To prohibit the National Science Foundation from conducting a study of the history of standards and standardization in the United States.

## § 1. Prohibition

The National Science Foundation shall not use or provide to others funds for conducting a study of the history of standards and standardization in the United States, including standards relating to uniform systems of weights, measures, timekeeping, electrical units, and product specifications governing everything from screw threads to paper sizes.
