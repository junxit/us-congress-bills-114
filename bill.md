# H.R. 414

> To amend the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act to repeal certain additional disclosure requirements, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Burdensome Data Collection Relief Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of additional disclosure requirements

Section 953(b) of the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act (15 U.S.C. 781 note) is hereby repealed and any regulations issued pursuant to such subsection shall have no force or effect.
