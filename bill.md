# H.R. 6249

> To amend the Food, Agriculture, Conservation, and Trade Act of 1990 to provide for a macadamia tree health initiative, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Macadamia Tree Health Initiative”.

## § 2. Macadamia tree health initiative

Section 1672(d) of the Food, Agriculture, Conservation, and Trade Act of 1990 (7 U.S.C. 5925(d)) is amended by adding at the end the following new paragraph:

> - **(11)** *Macadamia tree health initiative* Research and extension grants may be made under this section for the purposes of—
>   - **(A)** developing and disseminating science-based tools and treatments to combat the macadamia felted coccid (Eriococcus ironsidei); and
>   - **(B)** establishing an areawide integrated pest management program in areas affected by, or areas at risk of being affected by, the macadamia felted coccid.
