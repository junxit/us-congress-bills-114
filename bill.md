# S. 49

> To include a question to ascertain United States citizenship and immigration status in each questionnaire used for a decennial census of population, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Ascertaining citizenship and immigration status in decennial census of population

Section 141 of title 13, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating subsection (g) as subsection (h); and
- **(2)** by inserting after subsection (f) the following:
  > - **(g)** Notwithstanding section 5 of this title, the Secretary shall include in each questionnaire used for the conduct of a decennial census of population under subsection (a) a question to ascertain United States citizenship and immigration status.
