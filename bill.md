# H.R. 3789

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to furnish a memorial headstone or marker to commemorate an eligible individual whose remains are identified and available but the location of the gravesite is unknown.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Honor Our Fallen Heroes Act of 2015”.

## § 2. Department of Veterans Affairs headstones and markers for individuals whose remains are identified and available but the location of the gravesite is unknown

- **(a)** *In general* Section 2306(b)(1) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by inserting before the period at the end of the first sentence the following: “or an eligible individual whose remains are identified and available but the location of the gravesite is unknown”.
- **(b)** *Applicability* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to deaths occurring before, on, or after the date of the enactment of this Act.
