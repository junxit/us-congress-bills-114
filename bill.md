# S. 2816

> To reauthorize the diesel emissions reduction program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Diesel Emissions Reduction Act of 2016”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of diesel emissions reduction program

Section 797(a) of the Energy Policy Act of 2005 (42 U.S.C. 16137(a)) is amended by striking “2016” and inserting “2021”.
