# S.J. RES. 19

> To express the disfavor of Congress regarding the proposed agreement for cooperation between the United States and the People’s Republic of China transmitted to the Congress by the President on April 21, 2015, pursuant to the Atomic Energy Act of 1954.

That the Congress does not favor the proposed agreement for cooperation transmitted to the Congress by the President on April 21, 2015.
