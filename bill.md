# H.R. 1894

> To authorize the extension of preferential tariff treatment for certain textile goods imported from Morocco.

## § 1. Extension of Tariff Preference Level Program for Morocco

- **(a)** *In general* U.S. Note 64(b) to subchapter XII of chapter 99 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “shall be as follows:” and all that follows through “As used in this note” and inserting “shall be 10,000,000 SME for each of the calendar years 2016 through 2025. As used in this note”; and
  - **(2)** by striking “December 31, 2015” and inserting “December 31, 2025”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply with respect to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after January 1, 2016.
