# H. RES. 804

> Amending the Rules of the House of Representatives to allow independent, non-government television cameras to broadcast House floor proceedings.

That clause 2(b) of rule V of the Rules of the House of Representatives is amended by inserting before the period at the end thereof the following: “and shall be permitted to record and broadcast at any time a Member is present on the floor.”.
