# H.R. 1899

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to extend to all veterans with a serious service-connected injury eligibility to participate in the family caregiver services program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Caregivers Expansion and Improvement Act of 2015”.

## § 2. Extension to all veterans with a serious service-connected disability of eligibility for participation in family caregiver program

Section 1720G(a)(2)(B) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “on or after September 11, 2001”.
