# H.R. 6297

> To reauthorize the Iran Sanctions Act of 1996.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Iran Sanctions Extension Act”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of Iran Sanctions Act of 1996

Section 13(b) of the Iran Sanctions Act of 1996 (Public Law 104–172; 50 U.S.C. 1701 note) is amended by striking “December 31, 2016” and inserting “December 31, 2026”.
