# S. 2910

> To require the Secretary of Defense to implement processes and procedures to provide expedited treatment of fetal anomalies under the TRICARE program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “TRICARE Treatment for Fetal Repair Improvement Act of 2016”.

## § 2. Expedited treatment for fetal repair under the TRICARE program

- **(a)** *In general* The Secretary of Defense shall implement processes and procedures to ensure that a covered beneficiary under the TRICARE program who is diagnosed with a fetal anomaly receives evaluation and treatment from a qualified health care provider in the pediatric specialties of fetal medicine and surgery so that surgical repair of the fetal anomaly may occur in an expedited manner.
- **(b)** *Definitions* In this section, the terms covered beneficiary and TRICARE program have the meanings given those terms in section 1072 of title 10, United States Code.
