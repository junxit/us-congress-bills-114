# H.R. 354

> To impose a civil penalty against a railroad carrier when a shift change of train employees causes a blockage of vehicular traffic at a grade crossing.

## § 1. Grade crossing blockage penalty

Section 20134 of title 49, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new subsection:

> - **(d)** *Grade crossing blockage penalty* The Secretary shall assess a civil penalty against a railroad carrier in the amount of $10,000 for each complete hour in which a shift change of such carrier’s train employees causes a blockage of vehicular traffic at a grade crossing.
