# S. 877

> To establish a pilot grant program to assist State and local law enforcement agencies in purchasing body-worn cameras for law enforcement officers.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Police Creating Accountability by Making Effective Recording Available Act of 2015” or the “Police CAMERA Act”.
