# H.R. 2112

> To direct the United States Postal Service to designate a single, unique ZIP Code for North Plainfield, New Jersey.

## § 1. Single, unique ZIP Code for North Plainfield, New Jersey

Not later than 180 days after the date of the enactment of this section, the United States Postal Service shall designate a single, unique ZIP Code applicable to the area encompassing only North Plainfield, New Jersey.
