# H.R. 6044

> To limit the amount authorized to be appropriated to carry out chapter 2 of title IV of the Immigration and Nationality Act, relating to refugee resettlement.

## § 1. Authorization of appropriations

Section 414(a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1524(a)) is amended to read as follows:

> - **(a)** There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this chapter $1,192,000,000 for fiscal year 2017 and each subsequent fiscal year.
