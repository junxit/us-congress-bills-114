# S. 2688

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 615 6th Avenue SE in Cedar Rapids, Iowa, as the “Sergeant First Class Terryl L. Pasker Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Sergeant First Class Terryl L. Pasker Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 615 6th Avenue SE in Cedar Rapids, Iowa, shall be known and designated as the “Sergeant First Class Terryl L. Pasker Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Sergeant First Class Terryl L. Pasker Post Office Building”.
