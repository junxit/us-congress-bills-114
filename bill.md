# S. RES. 119

> Amending rule XXVIII of the Standing Rules of the Senate to provide for timely establishment of conference committees.

## § 1. Motions to go to conference

Rule XXVIII of the Standing Rules of the Senate is amended by striking paragraph 2(b) and inserting the following:

> - **(b)** Consideration of a motion described in subparagraph (a), including consideration of any debatable motion or appeal in connection therewith, shall be limited to not more than 2 hours.
