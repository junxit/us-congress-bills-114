# H.R. 4343

> To amend titles 23 and 49, United States Code, with respect to bikeshare projects, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Bikeshare Transit Act of 2016”.

## § 2. Definitions

Section 5302 of title 49, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (1)(E)—
  - **(A)** by striking “and installing” and inserting “, installing”; and
  - **(B)** by inserting before the semicolon the following: “, and bikeshare projects, including any project or activity carried out to facilitate a bicycle rental operation that makes bicycles available for public transportation, allows riders to pick up and drop off bicycles, serves a defined geographic area, and facilitates the transit of individuals between various points in such geographic area”; and
- **(2)** in paragraph (3)—
  - **(A)** in subparagraph (K) by striking “or” at the end;
  - **(B)** in subparagraph (L) by striking the period at the end and inserting “; or”; and
  - **(C)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(M)** the acquisition or replacement of bicycles and related equipment, including technology and vehicles needed to restock stations, and the construction of bicycle-related facilities to facilitate a bikeshare project.

## § 3. Congestion mitigation and air quality improvement program eligible projects

Section 149(b)(7) of title 23, United States Code, is amended by inserting “bikesharing,” after “carsharing,”.
