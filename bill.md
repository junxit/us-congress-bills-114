# H.R. 3777

> To provide for relief from sequester under the Balanced Budget and Emergency Deficit Control Act of 1985 and offsets to such relief through reforms in certain revenue and direct spending programs, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “America First Act”.
