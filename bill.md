# S.J. RES. 11

> Disapproving the action of the District of Columbia Council in approving the Human Rights Amendment Act of 2014.

That the Congress disapproves of the action of the District of Columbia Council described as follows: The Human Rights Amendment Act of 2014 (D.C. Act 20–605), signed by the Mayor of the District of Columbia on January 25, 2015, and transmitted to Congress pursuant to section 602(c)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act on March 6, 2015.
