# S. 372

> To ensure access to certain information for financial services industry regulators, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “SAFE Act Confidentiality and Privilege Enhancement Act”.

## § 2. Confidentiality of information shared between state and federal financial services regulators

Section 1512(a) of the S.A.F.E. Mortgage Licensing Act of 2008 (12 U.S.C. 5111(a)) is amended by inserting “or financial services” before “industry”.
