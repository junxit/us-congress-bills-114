# H.R. 6363

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend for two years the Indian employment credit.

## § 1. Extension of credit for Indian employment

- **(a)** *In general* Subsection (f) of section 45A of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “December 31, 2016” and inserting “December 31, 2018”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after December 31, 2016.
