# S. 823

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 206 West Commercial Street in East Rochester, New York, as the “Officer Daryl R. Pierson Post Office”.

## § 1. Officer Daryl R. Pierson Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 206 West Commercial Street in East Rochester, New York, shall be known and designated as the “Officer Daryl R. Pierson Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Officer Daryl R. Pierson Post Office”.
