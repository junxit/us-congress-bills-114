# H.R. 6304

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 501 North Main Street in Florence, Arizona, as the “Adolfo “Harpo” Celaya Post Office”.

## § 1. Adolfo “Harpo” Celaya Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 501 North Main Street in Florence, Arizona, shall be known and designated as the “Adolfo “Harpo” Celaya Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Adolfo “Harpo” Celaya Post Office”.
