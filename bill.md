# S. 1442

> To amend the Federal Crop Insurance Act to strike a provision relating to the budget neutrality of any renegotiated Standard Reinsurance Agreement.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Exclusion for Deficit Reduction in Agricultural Subsidies Elimination Act” or the “ERASE Act”.

## § 2. Renegotiation of Standard Reinsurance Agreement

Section 508(k)(8) of the Federal Crop Insurance Act (7 U.S.C. 1508(k)(8)) is amended by striking subparagraph (F).
