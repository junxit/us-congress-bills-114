# H.R. 782

> To eliminate the sequestration under section 251A of the Balanced Budget and Emergency Deficit Control Act of 1985, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Cancel the Sequester Act of 2015”.

## § 2. Repeal of 251A sequestration

Section 251A of the Balanced Budget and Emergency Deficit Control Act of 1985 (2 U.S.C. 901a) is repealed.
