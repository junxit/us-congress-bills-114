# H. CON. RES. 90

> Directing the Secretary of the Senate to make a technical correction in the enrollment of S. 1356.

That in the enrollment of the bill S. 1356, the Secretary of the Senate shall correct the title so as to read: “An Act to authorize appropriations for fiscal year 2016 for military activities of the Department of Defense, for military construction, and for defense activities of the Department of Energy, to prescribe military personnel strengths for such fiscal year, and for other purposes.”.
