# H.R. 4016

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the limitation on the carryover of excess corporate charitable contributions.

## § 1. Extension of limitation on carryover of excess corporate charitable contributions

- **(a)** *In general* Section 170(d)(2)(A) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “5 succeeding taxable years” and inserting “20 succeeding taxable years”, and
  - **(2)** by striking “the second, third, fourth, or fifth” and inserting “each of the second through twentieth”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to contributions made in taxable years beginning after December 31, 2009.
