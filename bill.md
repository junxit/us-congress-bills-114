# H.R. 5601

> To amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to require the label of any drug containing an opiate to prominently state that addiction is possible.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Carl’s Law”.

## § 2. Labeling requirement to alert all consumers of opiates that addiction is possible

- **(a)** *Misbranding* Section 502 of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 352) is amended by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(dd)** If it is a drug containing an opiate (as defined in section 102 of the Controlled Substances Act) unless the label prominently states that the drug contains an opiate and addiction is possible.

- **(b)** *Delayed applicability* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies beginning on the date that is 180 days after the date of enactment of this Act.
