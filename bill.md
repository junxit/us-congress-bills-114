# H.R. 5307

> To amend title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972 to define the term “sex” for purposes of such title.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Title IX Clarification Act of 2016”.

## § 2. Amendments

Section 901(c) of the Education Amendments of 1972 (20 U.S.C. 1681(c)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “title an educational institution” and inserting the following:
  > -  title—
  >   - **(1)** the term “educational institution” means

- **(2)** by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”, and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(2)** the term “sex” means with respect to an individual the biological sex of such individual.
