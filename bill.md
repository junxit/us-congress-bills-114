# H.R. 2549

> To amend the HITECH Act with respect to accessing, sharing, and using health data for research purposes.

## § 1. Accessing, sharing, and using health data for research purposes

- **(a)** *In general* The HITECH Act (title XIII of division A of Public Law 111–5) is amended by adding at the end of subtitle D of such Act (42 U.S.C. 17921 et seq.) the following:
- **(b)** *Revision of regulations* Not later than 12 months after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of Health and Human Services shall revise and clarify the provisions of title 45, Code of Federal Regulations, for consistency with part 4 of subtitle D of the HITECH Act, as added by subsection (a).
