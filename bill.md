# S. 1755

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide for a 5-year extension of the tax credit for residential energy efficient property.

## § 1. Extension of credit for residential energy efficient property

- **(a)** *In general* Subsection (g) of section 25D of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “December 31, 2016” and inserting “December 31, 2021”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after December 31, 2016.
