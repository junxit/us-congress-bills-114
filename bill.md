# H.R. 3004

> To amend the Gullah/Geechee Cultural Heritage Act to extend the authorization for the Gullah/Geechee Cultural Heritage Corridor Commission.

## § 1. Extension of the authorization for the Gullah/Geechee Cultural Heritage Corridor Commission

Section 295D(d) of the Gullah/Geechee Cultural Heritage Act (Public Law 109–338; 120 Stat. 1833; 16 U.S.C. 461 note) is amended by striking “10 years” and inserting “15 years”.
