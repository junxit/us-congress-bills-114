# H.R. 5041

> To prohibit the National Science Foundation from conducting a study to determine which facets of social interaction about politics are most stress inducing, for which kinds of people, and in which contexts.

## § 1. Prohibition

The National Science Foundation shall not use or provide to others funds for conducting a study to determine which facets of social interaction about politics are most stress inducing, for which kinds of people, and in which contexts.
