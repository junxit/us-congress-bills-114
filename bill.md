# H.R. 5596

> To amend the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 to provide that an issuer of municipal securities is not required to retain a municipal advisor prior to issuing any such securities.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Municipal Advisor Choice Act”.

## § 2. Requirement for municipal advisor for issuers of municipal securities

Section 15B(d) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (15 U.S.C. 78o–4(d)) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(3)** An issuer of municipal securities shall not be required to retain a municipal advisor prior to issuing any such securities.
