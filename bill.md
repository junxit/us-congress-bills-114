# H.R. 6355

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend for one year the credit for energy-efficient existing homes.

## § 1. Extension of credit for energy-efficient existing homes

- **(a)** *In general* Paragraph (2) of section 25C(g) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “December 31, 2016.” and inserting “December 31, 2017”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to property placed in service after December 31, 2016.
