# S. 77

> To repeal the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Patient Choice Restoration Act”.

## § 2. Repeal

- **(a)** *In general* The following Acts, and the amendments made by such Acts, are repealed:
  - **(1)** The Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act (Public Law 111–148).
  - **(2)** The Health Care and Education Reconciliation Act of 2010 (Public Law 111–152).
- **(b)** *Effect of law* The provisions of law amended by the Acts referred to in subsection (a) are restored as if such Acts had never been enacted.
