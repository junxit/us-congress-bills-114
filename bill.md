# H.R. 5835

> To repeal the minimum State allocation requirement for the Housing Trust Fund established under section 1338 of the Housing and Community Development Act of 1992, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “The Fair Distribution of Affordable Housing Funds Act of 2016”.

## § 2. Repeal of minimum State allocation requirement

Section 1338(c)(4) of the Housing and Community Development Act of 1992 (42 U.S.C. 4568(c)(4)) is amended by striking subparagraph (C).
