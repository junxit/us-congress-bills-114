# H.R. 2575

> To amend the Fair Minimum Wage Act of 2007 to postpone a scheduled increase in the minimum wage applicable to American Samoa.

## § 1. Minimum wage applicable to American Samoa

Section 8103(b)(2)(C) of the Fair Minimum Wage Act of 2007 (29 U.S.C. 206(b)(2)(C) note) is amended by striking “and 2014” and inserting “2014, 2015, and 2016”.
