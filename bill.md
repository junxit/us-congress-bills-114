# H.R. 5615

> To reinstate the ban on semiautomatic assault weapons.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Freedom From Fear Act”.

## § 2. Reinstatement of ban on semiautomatic assault weapons

Notwithstanding section 110105 of the Violent Crime Control and Law Enforcement Act of 1994 (Public Law 103–322), sections 110102 and 110106 of such Act are hereby re-enacted into law, except that, in lieu of the matter to be inserted by the amendment made by section 110102(c)(1) of such Act, the matter to be inserted shall be “(q), or (v) of section 922”.
