# H.R. 3902

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend for one year the credit for combined heat and power system property.

## § 1. Extension of credit for combined heat and power system property

- **(a)** *In general* Section 48(c)(3)(A)(iv) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “January 1, 2017” and inserting “January 1, 2018”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to property placed in service after December 31, 2016.
