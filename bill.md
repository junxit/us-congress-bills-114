# H.R. 2928

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 201 B Street in Perryville, Arkansas, as the “Harold George Bennett Post Office”.

## § 1. Harold George Bennett Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 201 B Street in Perryville, Arkansas, shall be known and designated as the “Harold George Bennett Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Harold George Bennett Post Office”.
