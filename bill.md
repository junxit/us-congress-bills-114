# H.R. 162

> To amend the Truth in Lending Act to allow certain loans that are not fully amortizing to be used in seller carryback financing on residential mortgage loans.

## § 1. Removing limitation

Section 103(cc)(2)(E) of the Truth in Lending Act (as added by section 1401 of the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking clause (ii); and
- **(2)** by redesignating clauses (iii), (iv), and (v) as clauses (ii), (iii), and (iv), respectively.
