# H.R. 5441

> To prohibit the National Endowment for the Arts to use funds to make grants for Literature Fellowships: Translation Projects.

## § 1. Prohibition on the use of funds by the National Endowment for the Arts for grants for Literature Fellowships: Translation Projects

No funds available to the National Endowment for the Arts may be used to make grants for Literature Fellowships: Translation Projects.
