# S. 1339

> To permanently authorize the special immigrant nonminister religious worker program.

## § 1. Special immigrant nonminister religious worker program

Section 101(a)(27)(C)(ii) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1101(a)(27)(C)(ii)) is amended—

- **(1)** in subclause (I), by striking the comma at the end and inserting a semicolon;
- **(2)** by striking “before September 30, 2015,” each place such phrase appears; and
- **(3)** in subclause (II), by striking “, or” and inserting “; or”.
