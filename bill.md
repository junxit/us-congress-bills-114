# H.R. 2345

> To extend the date after which interest earned on obligations held in the wildlife restoration fund may be available for apportionment.

## § 1. Interest on obligations held in the wildlife restoration fund

Section 3(b)(2)(C) of the Pittman-Robertson Wildlife Restoration Act (16 U.S.C. 669b(b)(2)(C)) is amended by striking “2016” and inserting “2026”.
