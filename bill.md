# H. RES. 351

> Expressing the sense of the House of Representatives regarding hydroelectric power.

That it is the sense of the House of Representatives that hydroelectric power is the most abundant source of clean, renewable energy in the United States and should be fully utilized in the Nation’s pursuit of energy independence and affordable energy for all people of the United States.
