# H.R. 1109

> To amend title II of the Social Security Act to increase the maximum amount of the lump-sum death benefit.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Social Security Lump-Sum Death Benefit Improvement and Modernization Act of 2015”.

## § 2. Increase in maximum amount of lump-sum death payments

- **(a)** *In general* Section 202(i) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 402(i)) is amended by striking “$255” and inserting “$1,000”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply with respect to deaths that occur after the date of the enactment of this Act.
