# H.R. 2574

> To amend the Fair Minimum Wage Act of 2007 to stop a scheduled increase in the minimum wage applicable to American Samoa and to provide that any future increases in such minimum wage shall be determined by the government of American Samoa.

## § 1. Minimum wage applicable to American Samoa

Section 8103(b)(2) of the Fair Minimum Wage Act of 2007 (29 U.S.C. 206(b)(2) note) is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (A)—
  - **(A)** by striking “on the date of enactment of this Act” and inserting “May 1, 2015”; and
  - **(B)** by inserting “and” after the semicolon; and
- **(2)** by striking subparagraphs (B) and (C) and inserting the following:
  > - **(B)** increased thereafter by such amount and at such time as determined by the government of American Samoa.
