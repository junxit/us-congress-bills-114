# H.R. 6014

> To allow the Administrator of the Federal Aviation Administration to enter into reimbursable agreements for certain airport projects.

## § 1. Reimbursable agreements for certain airport projects

The Administrator of the Federal Aviation Administration may enter into a reimbursable agreement with a State or local government agency to carry out a project at an airport as to which notice is required under section 77.9 of title 14, Code of Federal Regulations, if the agreement—

- **(1)** includes measures for cost-effective completion of such project; and
- **(2)** would not negatively affect the safety or efficiency of the national airspace system.
