# H.R. 1721

> To reauthorize appropriations for the National Women’s Rights History Project Act.

## § 1. Reauthorization of Appropriations

- **(a)** *Votes for Women’s Trail* Section 1602 of Title XVI of Public Law 96–607 (16 U.S.C. 410ll–1) is amended in subsection (f)(2) by striking “2013” and inserting “2019”.
- **(b)** *National Women’s Rights History Project Registry and the National Women’s Rights History Project Partnership Network* Section 7111 of the Omnibus Public Land Management Act of 2009 (Public Law 111–11) is amended—
  - **(1)** in subsection (b)(5) by striking “2013” and inserting “2019”; and
  - **(2)** in subsection (c)(4) by striking “2013” and inserting “2019”.
