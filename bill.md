# S. 3246

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to extend authority for operation of the Department of Veterans Affairs Regional Office in Manila, the Republic of the Philippines.

## § 1. Extension of authority for operation of the Department of Veterans Affairs Regional Office in Manila, the Republic of the Philippines

Section 315(b) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “September 30, 2016” and inserting “September 30, 2017”.
